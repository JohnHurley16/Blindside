# The vision board

Every art concept in the game, surface and underground: at least one view that shows the
thing and at least one in the game's own light. Rendered 2026-09-08. Open `index.html`
beside this file to scroll the whole board as one contact sheet.

**Most of this is proposal, not settled design.** Each group's `NOTES.md` says, per image,
what it draws that the documents fix and what it invents, and ends with every guess in one
list. Strike them there.


---


230 images, 54 concepts. 31 have both a clear and an in-situ view; 23 have only one. 62 further renders are uncaptioned.
